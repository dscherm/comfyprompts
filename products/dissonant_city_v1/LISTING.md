## Full description (paste, markdown)
> **DissonantCity** is a 29-piece retro-futurist neon city kit in a synthwave
> aesthetic — dark-navy flat-shaded bodies wrapped in glowing pink/cyan neon
> edge-outlines and window grids.
>
> **Towers:** tall cyan, tall purple, short pink, cylinder, spiral, prism, neon.
> **Landmarks:** dome building, arcology, ziggurat, slab shops, skybridge + supports.
> **Ground:** straight / corner / junction roads, plaza tiles.
> **Props:** streetlight, billboard, neon arch, hover cars, antenna, holo-pylon,
> fountain pad, crystals, retro palm, barrier.
>
> Modular on a 2-unit grid — roads and ground tiles snap edge-to-edge, towers and
> props drop straight on. Neon is emissive, so it blooms in-engine. **GLB + OBJ +
> FBX**, working in Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, and Blender. Includes a ready-to-run
> Godot 4 example project (piece browser + full assembled city).
>
> Procedurally generated low-poly — minimal AI involvement (Blender geometry, not
> AI image-gen).
