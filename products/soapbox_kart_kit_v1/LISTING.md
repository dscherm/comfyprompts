## Full description (paste, markdown)
> **Soapbox Kart Kit** is a complete cartoon soapbox-racing set: chunky, saturated,
> and game-ready.
>
> **Karts (4):** classic racer, rocket kart, bathtub tub, wooden crate racer.
> **Track (7):** straight / corner / start-line tiles, up-ramp, jump ramp, finish
> gate, checkpoint arch.
> **Hazards & props (12):** cones, tire stacks, crates, striped barriers, barrels,
> hay bales, oil slicks, puddles, boost pad, arrow signs, checkered flag, banner.
> **Pickups (3):** glowing boost, shield, and wrench icons.
> **Mascot racers (5, textured):** robot, frog, wizard, shark, skeleton — each a
> character seated in a soapbox kart (AI-generated then cleaned to game-ready meshes).
>
> Kit pieces are flat-shaded low-poly on a 2-unit grid — tiles snap edge-to-edge,
> props drop straight on. **GLB + OBJ + FBX**, working in Godot 4, Unity, Unreal,
> and Blender.
>
> Procedural pieces are original Blender geometry; the 5 mascot racers are
> AI-generated (Flux + Hunyuan3D) from original prompts — disclosed AI, no third-party IP.
