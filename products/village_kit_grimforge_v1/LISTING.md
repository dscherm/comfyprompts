## Full description (paste, markdown)
> **GrimForge Village** is a low-poly modular kit for grim medieval villages —
> 28 hand-tuned pieces in a cohesive dark-fantasy palette.
>
> **Buildings (8):** cottage, small house, tall house, tavern, church, barn,
> watchtower, blacksmith — with Tudor timber framing, overhanging upper floors,
> gable roofs, stone chimneys, and glowing windows.
> **Walls (3):** crenellated wall, gatehouse, corner. **Paths/ground (4):** grass,
> dirt, cobble straight & corner. **Props (13):** well, market stall, barrel,
> crate, fence, tree, dead tree, lamppost, glowing brazier, signpost, cart,
> haystack, gravestone.
>
> **Grid-modular** (1-unit), centered at origin, base at floor level — snap walls
> and paths, rotate in 90° steps. **GLB + OBJ + FBX** included. Solid-color
> flat-shaded — no textures to break across engines; brazier/windows are emissive.
>
> Works in **Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, Blender**. Procedurally generated low-poly.
> Pairs with the GrimForge art LoRA + texture pack.
