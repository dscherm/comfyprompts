## Full description (paste, markdown)
> **Fantasy RPG Tileset — Vol. 1** — 65 hand-tuned 16×16 tiles for top-down RPGs.
>
> **Includes:**
> - **11 seamless terrain tiles** — grass (+2 variants for variation),
>   flowers/dark grass, dirt, water, deep water, stone, sand, cobblestone path.
> - **48 Wang autotile transitions** — grass→dirt, grass→water, grass→sand
>   (16 corner-tiles each) for clean, connected terrain borders.
> - **6 object sprites** (transparent bg) — tree, bush, rock, flowers, mushroom,
>   signpost.
>
> **Ships with:** a packed `atlas.png` (128×256, power-of-2), every tile as an
> individual PNG, a `metadata.json` (regions + autotile corner masks), a sample
> map, and an overview sheet.
>
> **Engine-ready:** Godot 4 (TileSet/Terrain), Unity (2D Tilemap / Rule Tiles),
> or any engine via the metadata. 16×16 native — scale with nearest-neighbor.
>
> Cohesive 16-bit palette. Procedurally generated pixel art.
