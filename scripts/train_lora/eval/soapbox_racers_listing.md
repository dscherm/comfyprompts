## Description (paste)
> **soapbox_racers** is a Flux.1-dev LoRA for a **gritty comic-book / ink
> illustration** character style: heavy black outlines, dense ink, crosshatching,
> realistic proportions, big expressive faces, muted wasteland palette. Influences:
> 1960s Hanna-Barbera action, Speed Racer, Mad Max: Fury Road, R. Crumb (Zap Comix),
> Katsuhiro Otomo (Akira), Jack Davis (Mad Magazine).
>
> **Trigger:** `soapbox_racers`
> **Recommended strength:** **1.1** (0.9 lighter · 1.3 max-bold)
> **Prompt pattern:** `soapbox_racers, flat ink comic book illustration, bold black outlines, <your character>, standing, full body, white background`
> **Tip:** for clean character art, keep "flat ink comic, white background" in the
> prompt; avoid "wasteland/desert racer" alone (it can pull photoreal vehicles).
>
> Trained on an original cast (the *Soapbox Sabotage* game) and proven across 9
> distinct characters — skeletal reaper, mechanic, desert warrior, scavenger kid,
> wasteland queen, ironclad, gas-mask chemist, livewire, and the rookie.
>
